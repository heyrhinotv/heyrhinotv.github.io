    Where these numbers come from
    1. The 17–19 hours vs 0.05% blood alcohol, and the most alcohol given (about 0.1%): 39 people, some tests, not all. Williamson & Feyer, Occup Environ Med 2000
    2. Alarm center (amygdala) about 60% more reactive after one night without sleep, small brain-scan study: Yoo et al., Current Biology 2007 · UC Berkeley
    3. New memories saved up to 40% worse after one night without sleep: Yoo et al., Nature Neuroscience 2007 · Harvard Gazette
    4. About 1 hour of sleep debt took 4 days to recover, 15 young men in a lab: Kitamura et al., Scientific Reports 2016
    5. Sleeping in on the weekend didn't undo short nights' effects on metabolism: Depner et al., Current Biology 2019
    6. Drivers who slept 4–5 hours: about 4× (4.3×) the crash rate of drivers who slept 7 or more: AAA Foundation for Traffic Safety 2016
    7. Hunger up 24% after two short nights, 12 young men: Spiegel et al., Annals of Internal Medicine 2004
    8. Adults 18–60: 7 or more hours a night: Watson et al., AASM + Sleep Research Society consensus, 2015
